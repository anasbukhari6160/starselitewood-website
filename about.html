Who We Are

Your Direct Manufacturer for Wood Packaging

Stars Elite Wood Industry LLC — a Sharjah-based manufacturer of wooden pallets, crates, and timber. We focus on reducing supply risk and delivering export-ready products with the documentation you need.

Our Story

Built on Experience,
Driven by Quality

Stars Elite Wood Industry LLC was established in 2024 with a clear goal: to be the manufacturer that procurement teams and importers call when they need reliable wooden pallets, crates, or timber — with no middleman and no surprises on compliance.

Our team combines experienced production and management. We invest in modern machinery and processes so we can deliver the specs you need, in the quantities you need, with ISPM-15 treatment and documentation when required for export.

We focus on B2B: logistics, manufacturing, and export businesses that need a direct supplier they can trust for quality, lead times, and customs-ready documentation.
