Our Story

Built on Experience,
Driven by Quality

Started in the beginning of 2024, Stars Elite Wood Industry LLC was founded with a clear vision: to become the most reliable wood products manufacturer and supplier that businesses can call first when they need pallets, crates, or timber.

Our management and production lines feature competent builders who are led by a core team of dedicated and highly experienced staff who have consistently proven themselves in promoting the growth and success of the company.

We have committed to providing quality products and customized services, making us the high-value supplier our customers call first. To ensure the highest quality, our factory is equipped with the latest and most comprehensive production systems and machineries for various kinds of wooden product manufacturing.
