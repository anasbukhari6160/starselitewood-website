International Standard

What Is ISPM-15?

ISPM-15 (International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15) is an international standard that regulates the treatment and marking of wood packaging materials used in international trade.

The standard aims to prevent the international transport and spread of disease and insects that could harm forest ecosystems around the world. Most countries require wood packaging material to be ISPM-15 compliant.

All our treated products carry the official IPPC mark — a universally recognized symbol that guarantees compliance with international phytosanitary regulations.

Accepted by importing countries worldwide
Prevents spread of invasive insects and pathogens
Required for most international wood packaging export
IPPC stamp provides visible compliance confirmation
