Wooden Pallets

We manufacture and supply wooden pallets in Two Way (Stringer) and Four Way (Block) configurations — custom-sized and compliant with international shipping standards.

Two Way Entry

Two Way Wooden Pallet
(Stringer Pallet)

Also known as a Stringer Pallet, it uses "stringers" to support the unit load. Can be lifted by two sides (front and back) using hand jack or forklift. Built with top deck, stringer, and bottom deck. Custom sizes and ISPM-15 treatment available.

  • Two-way entry — front and back forklift/hand jack access
  • Cost-effective for standard warehouse flows
  • Custom dimensions to fit your racking and loads
  • ISPM-15 heat treatment for export
EntryTwo way (stringer length sides)
ConstructionTop deck, stringers, bottom deck
Typical useWarehousing, distribution, domestic & export
TreatmentHeat treatment / fumigation on request
Use cases / Industries

Logistics & warehousing, FMCG, manufacturing, import/export. Ideal where two-way entry is sufficient and cost is a priority.

FAQ
What sizes do you offer?
We manufacture to your dimensions. Common sizes include 1200×1000mm and 48×40 inch; specify your required L×W×H.
Is ISPM-15 treatment available?
Yes. We can heat treat or fumigate and provide IPPC stamping for export.

Four Way Entry

Four Way Wooden Pallet
(Block Pallet)

A true 4-way pallet using solid wood blocks to support the unit load. Can be lifted from all four sides with forklifts and hand jacks. Available with or without bottom deck; custom dimensions and ISPM-15 treatment available.

  • Four-way entry — maximum warehouse flexibility
  • Block construction for strength and durability
  • Ideal for automated and high-throughput facilities
  • Export-ready with heat treatment and IPPC stamping
EntryFour way (all sides)
ConstructionBlocks, top deck; optional bottom deck / perimeter base
Typical useWarehousing, export, automated handling
TreatmentHeat treatment / fumigation on request
Use cases / Industries

Distribution centers, 3PLs, manufacturing, retail. Best where pallets are handled from multiple directions or with pallet jacks from any side.

FAQ
Block vs stringer — which do I need?
Four-way (block) allows entry from any side and suits busy warehouses; two-way (stringer) is often more economical where only two-way entry is used.
Can you do custom block layouts?
Yes. We configure block count and deck layout to your load and handling requirements.
