Wooden Pallets

We manufacture and supply wooden pallets in Two Way (Stringer) and Four Way (Block) configurations — custom-sized and compliant with international shipping standards.

Two Way Entry

Two Way Wooden Pallet
(Stringer Pallet)

Also known as a Stringer Pallet, it uses "stringers" to support the unit load. Can be lifted by two sides (front and back) using lifting tools such as hand jack or fork-lift. Built with a frame of 3 parts: top deck, stringer, and bottom deck. When specifying, the length of the stringer is noted first, then the width of the deck boards.

Four Way Entry

Four Way Wooden Pallet
(Block Pallet)

A true 4-way pallet that uses blocks of solid wood to support the unit load. Typically uses four to twelve blocks to support the top deckboards. Can be easily lifted in all four dimensions (front, back, left, right) using fold-lifts and hand jacks. Can be designed with or without bottom deckboards, or a full perimeter base. Between the blocks and deckboards are thin stringers that form a mat with the deckboard.
